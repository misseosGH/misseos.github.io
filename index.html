🎣 The Pond

Fishing commands share one cooldown per viewer. It starts at 10 minutes and falls as low as 5 minutes with upgraded reels. !fish is always free. Higher tier bait improves rarity odds, rods improve weight luck, and Pond Score is lifetime progression that is never spent.

🎣 Fishing & Bait

!fish - Cast into The Pond with free Basic Bait.
!fishgood - Cast using one Good Bait for improved rarity odds.
!fishbetter - Cast using one Better Bait for stronger rarity odds.
!fishbest - Cast using one Best Bait for the strongest rarity odds.
!buygoodbait (1-10) - Buy Good Bait for 50 points each.
!buybetterbait (1-10) - Buy Better Bait for 150 points each.
!buybestbait (1-10) - Buy Best Bait for 350 points each.
!bait - Check how much Good, Better and Best Bait you own.

🧪 Alchemy

Alchemy is a collectible potion mini game. Potions are not consumable, so once brewed they stay in your Potion Journal. Ingredients come from The Pond, Bug Catching, Foraging, Expeditions and Bosses/Chests. Every potion recipe uses exactly five ingredients. Use the recipe browser below to find the potion you want, then click its name to copy it.

💬 Alchemy Commands

!brew (rarity potion) - Brew a potion when you own all five required ingredients.
!recipe (rarity potion) - Show the five ingredients needed for a potion.
!potions - View your Potion Journal summary.
!potions (rarity potion) - Check a specific potion in your Potion Journal.

🎲 Forest Games

Gamble and Coin Flip accept wagers from 10 to 5,000 points. Duels let viewers challenge each other, and Forest Slots has its own stats and leaderboard.

🎰 Forest Slots Big prizes, jackpot wins.

!slots - Spin the Forest Slots for 100 points.
!slotstats - View your personal Slots stats and records.
!topslots - Show the Slots leaderboard.
!topslots wins - Rank viewers by Slots wins.
!topslots won - Rank viewers by total points won from Slots.
!topslots net - Rank viewers by net Slots points.
!topslots biggest - Rank viewers by biggest single Slots win.
!topslots jackpots - Rank viewers by jackpots won.

⚔️ Duels

!duel (amount) - Open a duel that any eligible viewer can accept.
!duel @username (amount) - Challenge a specific viewer to a duel.
!duel accept - Accept the current eligible duel challenge.
!duel decline - Decline a targeted duel challenge.
!duel cancel - Cancel a duel you created.

🎲 Gamble & Coin Flip

!gamble (amount) - Gamble points for a chance to lose, break even or win 1.5×.
!gamble all - Gamble your available points up to the 5,000 point limit.
!coinflip heads (amount) - Choose heads and wager points for double or nothing.
!coinflip tails (amount) - Choose tails and wager points for double or nothing.
!coinflip heads all - Choose heads and wager your available points up to 5,000.
!coinflip tails all - Choose tails and wager your available points up to 5,000.

🌲 Forest Events & Loot

Some events start automatically while the stream is live. When one opens, jump in for rewards, prizes and a little forest chaos.

🎁 Random Drops

A quick reaction event. A hidden reward drops into chat and the first eligible viewer to claim it wins.

!claim - Claim the active Random Drop. First eligible viewer to claim it gets the hidden reward.

🧠 Trivia

A fast trivia round. Be the first to answer correctly to win points and build your trivia stats.

!triviastats - View your trivia wins, streaks, fastest answer and best category.
!toptrivia - Show the top five viewers by total trivia wins.

🔨 Forest Auctions

A major bidding event where viewers compete for a mystery reward. Keep an eye on the current leader and bid before time runs out.

!auction - Check the current Forest Auction, leader, next bid and time remaining.
!bid (amount) - Bid a whole number of points on the active Forest Auction.

🌲 Forest Expeditions

A major group adventure into the forest. Risk some points to join, then the expedition faces a story outcome with a chance at bigger rewards.

!explore (100-5000) - Join an active Expedition by risking between 100 and 5,000 points.

📦 Mystery Boxes

Mystery Boxes can drop from Forest activities and hold surprise rewards. Save them up or open them whenever you fancy.

!mysteryboxes - Check how many Mystery Boxes you own.
!openbox - Open one of your Mystery Boxes.
