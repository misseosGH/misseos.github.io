💰 Points, The Pond & Games

💰 Earning & Managing Points

Earn points while watching, extra points for chatting. Checking in grants a bonus 100 points & Subscribers receive a 1.2x watch-time multiplier.

!points - Check your points balance.
!points (username) - Check another viewer's points balance.
!toppoints - Show the top five point holders.
!givepoints @username (amount) - Give another viewer at least 1,000 points.
!givepoints @username all - Give another viewer all of your available points.

🎣 The Pond

Every fishing command shares a 10-minute cooldown per user. !fish is always free and uses Basic Bait. Good, Better and Best Bait are bought with points and one bait is consumed each time you use its matching fishing command. Better bait increases your chances of finding rarer creatures and treasure, but it does not affect catch weight. Pond Score tracks every point earned through The Pond and is not reduced when you spend points.

Fishing & Bait

!fish - Cast into The Pond with free Basic Bait.
!fishgood - Cast using one Good Bait.
!fishbetter - Cast using one Better Bait.
!fishbest - Cast using one Best Bait.
!buygoodbait (1-10) - Buy Good Bait for 50 points each.
!buybetterbait (1-10) - Buy Better Bait for 150 points each.
!buybestbait (1-10) - Buy Best Bait for 350 points each.
!bait - Check your Good, Better and Best Bait totals.

🎲 Point Games

The minimum wager is 10 points and the maximum is 5,000. Using all wagers your available balance. Gamble has a 40% loss, 20% draw and 40% win chance. A Gamble win returns 1.5x your wager in total, while a Coin Flip win returns 2x your wager in total.

!gamble (amount) - Gamble an amount for a chance to lose, break even or win 1.5x.
!gamble all - Gamble your available points up to the 5,000-point limit.
!coinflip heads (amount) - Choose heads and wager an amount for double or nothing.
!coinflip tails (amount) - Choose tails and wager an amount for double or nothing.
!coinflip heads all - Choose heads and wager your available points up to 5,000.
!coinflip tails all - Choose tails and wager your available points up to 5,000.
